The daily-streak flame with consecutive-day count. Persimmon when active, gold at-risk, grey when broken.

```jsx
<StreakCounter days={28} />
<StreakCounter days={6} size="lg" state="risk" />
<StreakCounter days={0} state="off" showLabel={false} />
```

Props: `days`, `size` (sm/md/lg), `state` (active/risk/off), `showLabel`.
